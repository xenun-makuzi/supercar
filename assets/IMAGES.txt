IMAGE SLOTS. Replace the supplied placeholder files with your own assets using the same name and extension.
hero/: four separate wide hero slides; use landscape images around 1920x1000 or larger. The homepage slides one at a time from right to left.
brands/: four manufacturer hero images per marque, ideally about 1400x900. Use <brand-slug>-1.jpg through <brand-slug>-4.jpg (for example ferrari-1.jpg to ferrari-4.jpg). logos/: transparent PNG files, around 600x300. parts/ and models/: landscape images around 800x600.
You can also point image_url, logo_url or hero_url in Supabase (or settings.hero_images) at hosted images instead. settings.hero_images should be a JSON array of up to four image URLs.
